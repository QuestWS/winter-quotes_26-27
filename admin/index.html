Unit details & storage

Yard alert

The one thing somebody must know before they touch this boat — shown on the crew's list, at the top of the unit in the yard app, and on the printed sheets. Keep it to one line, and clear it when it stops being true. The customer never sees it.

Yard status

Drives the three lists in the yard app. Mark a unit dropped off when the customer brings it in — that is what puts it on the crew's To store list without it ever having been in the water.

Yard log

Observations from the yard, newest last — written from the yard app or here. Append-only: entries are never edited or deleted, so two people can log the same boat without overwriting each other. The customer never sees this.

Staff notes

Why this quote is the way it is — discounts, odd dimensions, what was agreed on the phone. The customer never sees this. It is not on the PDF and not in any email.

Penalties

Charges the customer is never offered on the quote page. Apply one when it has been earned; removing it puts the total straight back. Nobody is emailed either way.

Automatic customer emails

Two emails send on their own: the 10-day quote reminder and the 24-hour lead follow-up. Pausing stops both. Everything staff send by hand from this console keeps working.

Load from an old sheet

Reads one of the old per-customer winter-services spreadsheets and turns it into a quote here, priced at today's rates. Nothing is written until you press Import, and no customer is emailed.

Re-price at current rates

Existing quotes keep the price they were saved at. This shows what each one becomes at today's rates, and lets you write the ones you choose. Deposits are left alone — the balance moves. No customer is emailed.

Send to all

Seasonal announcements that go to every customer at once. No quote needs to be open. Nothing sends until you have seen who it is going to and confirmed.

Restore from a backup

Upload one of the nightly backup attachments (the .xlsx file emailed each evening). Nothing changes until you choose what to restore, and a snapshot of the sheet as it is now is saved to Drive first.
